Health & Wellness

Take care of your whole self

Wellness is more than physical health. It also includes your mental, emotional, and everyday wellbeing.

🫀

Physical Wellbeing

Support your body with regular movement, balanced meals, hydration, preventive care, and enough rest.

🧠

Mental Wellbeing

Make space for calm moments, manage everyday stress, and reach out to people you trust when you need support.

🌿

Emotional Balance

Recognise your feelings, practise self-kindness, and build meaningful connections with others.

😴

Sleep & Rest

Create a relaxing bedtime routine and aim for a regular sleep schedule that helps you feel refreshed.

💧

Daily Hydration

Drink water regularly throughout the day, adjusting your needs for activity, climate, and personal health.

☀️

Mindful Living

Slow down when possible, enjoy the present moment, and make time for activities that bring you joy.

Small steps matter

Healthy habits for everyday life

You do not have to change everything at once. Start with one or two realistic habits and build gradually.

✓
Move regularly

Choose comfortable activities you enjoy and reduce long periods of sitting.

✓
Eat a variety of nourishing foods

Include fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and suitable sources of protein.

✓
Prioritise quality sleep

Keep a consistent bedtime and create a quiet, comfortable sleep environment.

✓
Make time to relax

Try deep breathing, a favourite hobby, or a few quiet minutes away from screens.

✓
Stay connected

Spend time with supportive friends and family, and ask for help when needed.
